At Duradent we take care of your personal data. This policy explains what data we process, why, who we share it with and how to exercise your rights, under **Law No. 29733, Peru's Personal Data Protection Law**, and its Regulations.

## 1. Data controller

**Duradent S.A.C.**, Av. Emancipación 271, stores 108 and 126, Cercado de Lima, Peru. Phone: [+51 933 741 379](tel:+51933741379).

## 2. What data we process

| Category | Data | When |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Name, email address, preferred language, password (stored encrypted) or Google / Microsoft identifier | When you sign up or sign in |
| Billing | Document type and number (DNI, RUC, foreign resident card), company name, professional registration number (optional), phone | When you complete your profile |
| Delivery | Delivery addresses, recipient's name, contact phone | When you add addresses |
| Orders and payments | Products, amounts, order status, tax documents and payment references | When you buy |
| Complaints Book | Name, ID document, address, phone, email and the details of your claim or complaint | When you file a complaint sheet |
| Technical | IP address, browser, cookies needed for the session, the cart and the language | When you use the store |

**We do not store your card details**: Culqi receives and processes them directly. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## 3. What we use it for

- Creating and managing your account and applying the price list that applies to you.
- Processing your orders, payments, deliveries, exchanges and returns.
- Issuing your electronic tax documents and meeting our tax obligations.
- Emailing you about your account and orders (email confirmation, order received, payment, shipping, tax document, refund).
- Answering your questions and complaints.
- Protecting the store and our customers against unauthorised access and fraud.

We process your data because it is necessary for the business relationship you request, to meet legal obligations and, where applicable, with your consent. We do not send advertising without your prior consent.

## 4. Who we share it with

We only share the data needed with providers that serve us and with the authorities required by law:

- **Culqi:** card and Yape payment processing.
- **Electronic invoicing provider and SUNAT:** issuing and filing tax documents.
- **Courier companies:** delivering your orders (recipient's name, address and phone).
- **Amazon Web Services:** hosting the store and sending emails.
- **Google or Microsoft:** only if you choose to sign in with them.

We do not sell or rent your data.

## 5. International transfer

The store and its emails are hosted on Amazon Web Services servers in the United States, so your data is transferred outside Peru. This provider applies appropriate security and confidentiality measures to protect the information.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Account data, while your account remains active.
- Orders, tax documents and payment records, for the period required by tax and accounting rules.
- Complaints Book sheets and their answers, for the period its regulations require.
- Technical security logs, for a limited period.

Afterwards we delete or anonymise it.

## 7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store passwords with strong hashing algorithms, limit access to data to the staff who need it and protect sign-in attempts against abuse.

## 8. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of **access, rectification, cancellation and objection** (ARCO rights) and withdraw your consent. You can see and correct much of your data directly in **My account**. For anything else, call us on [+51 933 741 379](tel:+51933741379) or visit our store; we will ask you to verify your identity and respond within the time limits set by law.

If you believe we did not handle your request properly, you may file a claim with Peru's **National Authority for Personal Data Protection** (Ministry of Justice and Human Rights).

## 9. Minors

The store is intended for professionals and companies. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will publish the current version on this page with its update date and, if the change is significant, we will let you know by email.
