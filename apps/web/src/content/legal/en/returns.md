We want you to receive exactly what you need. Because we sell products for clinical use, some conditions exist to protect hygiene and patient safety.

## 1. Cancelling an order

- **Before paying:** cancel it from **My orders**. If it is not paid in time, it is cancelled automatically.
- **Paid and not yet dispatched:** call us and we cancel it with a **full refund**.
- **Already dispatched:** the returns policy on this page applies.

## 2. Defective, damaged or wrong product

If you receive a product with a manufacturing defect, damaged in transit, different from what you ordered or incomplete:

- Tell us within **7 calendar days** of delivery, with your order number and photos of the product and the packaging.
- We offer an **exchange** for the same product or, if it is out of stock, a **full refund**.
- **Duradent pays** for the pickup or the new delivery.

Instruments (pliers, tweezers and similar) also carry the manufacturer's warranty against manufacturing defects; we will help you claim it.

## 3. Exchanges and returns for other reasons

If you ordered the wrong size or reference, or no longer need the product, we accept the return when:

- You request it within **7 calendar days** of delivery.
- The product is **unopened, in its original sealed packaging** and in the same condition you received it.
- You include the order number.

In these cases the return shipping cost is yours, or you can bring the product to our store.

## 4. Products we cannot take back

For health reasons we **do not accept returns** of products that have been opened, used or sterilised: brackets, tubes, bands, archwires, elastics, ligatures, adhesives and other supplies that come into contact with patients. This does not apply if the product arrived defective or wrong (section 2).

## 5. How to request it

1. Call us on [+51 933 741 379](tel:+51933741379) with your order number.
2. We will tell you how to send or bring the product.
3. We inspect the product within 3 business days of receiving it and confirm the outcome.

## 6. Refunds

- Refunds go back to the **same payment method**:
  - **Card or Yape:** we reverse the charge through Culqi; how soon you see it depends on your bank.
  - **Transfer:** we deposit into the bank account you give us, in the name of the order holder.
- We issue the **electronic credit note** that cancels the tax document.
- We make the refund within **5 business days** of approving the return.
- For partial returns we refund the value of the returned products. Delivery costs are refunded only if the error or defect was ours.

## 7. Your rights

This policy does not limit the rights granted to you by Peru's Consumer Protection and Defense Code (Law No. 29571) where applicable.
